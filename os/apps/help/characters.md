# Characters
Every emoji and symbol, found by name.

## Find one
Type what you're looking for, like "heart", "arrow" or "euro", or open a drawer on the left.

## Copy it
Click a character to copy it, then paste it anywhere with Ctrl+V. It joins Recent.

## Skin tone
For people and hands, pick a skin tone with the round swatches; Characters remembers it.

## Star the ones you use most
Star one and it stays in Starred.
