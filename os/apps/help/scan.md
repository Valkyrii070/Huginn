# Scan
Scan documents, or straighten photos of pages, into PDFs you can search.

## From a scanner
Scan finds scanners on their own (USB and on the network). Pick one, then choose Colour, Grey or Black and White and how much detail (300 dpi suits documents, 600 photos). A scanner with a feeder scans every page in it, on one side or both. Each scan becomes a page in the strip on the left.

## From photos
No scanner? Take a photo of the page with your phone and press Add Photos (or drop the photos on the window): Scan finds the paper's four corners and straightens it, as if it had been scanned. If it guessed wrong, press Edges and drag the corners onto the paper's corners; Whole Picture keeps it all.

## Cleaning up
Document makes the paper white and the ink black, and takes shadows away. Grey and Colour even out the light. As It Is leaves the photo alone; "Clean up every page this way" does the same to them all. Turn a page with the arrows. Nothing is lost: each page keeps its original, so any change can be undone or changed again.

## Pages
Drag pages in the strip to put them in order; the cross takes one away. Rename gives the document its name.

## Reading the text
Read This Page or Read All reads what's written (Tesseract does the reading, on this computer). Copy All the Text, or leave "Text you can search and copy" on: the PDF then has the words hidden behind the pictures, so it can be searched and copied from in any PDF reader.

## Saving
Save PDF makes a PDF in Documents/Scans, never over another file: A4, Letter or each page's own size. Save as Pictures keeps them as pictures. Show the Scans Folder opens it.
