# Shortcuts
Little recipes that do several things for you.

## Make one
Press New Shortcut, or start from a ready-made one. Pick when it runs: when you press it, at a time, when you log in, when a drive or phone is plugged in, when you join a Wi-Fi network, or when the battery runs low.

## Add steps
Press Add a step: open an app, a website or a file; show a notice; wait; copy new photos and videos from a camera or phone into Pictures/Imported; run a backup; set the volume; turn Wi-Fi on or off; or run a command. Steps run from the top; move them up and down.

## Run one
Press ▶ on its card, or switch on "In the apps menu" to find it with your apps.
