# Photos
Your pictures, by month, by album and favourites. Edit any picture without ever losing the original.

## Look
Click a photo to see it large; zoom with the wheel, go to the next one and see its details.

## Edit
Open a picture and choose Edit. Adjust has Light (exposure, brightness, contrast, highlights, shadows, black point), Colour (saturation, vibrance, warmth, tint) and Detail (sharpen, vignette, grain, fade). Auto Enhance makes a good start. Double-click a slider to put it back.

## Filters
Eleven looks, from Vivid to Noir and Golden Hour, each previewed on your picture. Strength turns one down.

## Crop and straighten
Choose a shape (free, square, 4:3, 16:9 and more) and drag the corners or the frame. Straighten levels a tilted horizon and trims the edges so no empty corners show. Turn and Flip are here too.

## Compare, undo and finish
Hold the eye to see the original. Undo and Redo step through your changes. Done saves; Save as Copy leaves the picture as it was and saves a new one beside it.

## The original is always kept
The first time a picture is edited, its original is put aside. Editing again starts from the original, so nothing wears down. Revert to Original brings it back at any time.

## Tidy
Rotate, add to favourites, send by Mail, or move to the Trash.
