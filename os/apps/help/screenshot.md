# Screenshot
Pictures and videos of your screen.

## Take a picture
Choose the whole screen, or let the desktop ask: the screen holds still, and you drag over the part you want (or take it all). Add a delay to set things up first.

## Mark it up
Pen, box and highlighter in six colours, with undo. Save it or copy it.

## Record a video
Record a Video asks the desktop what to share, then records it to Videos/Screen Recordings, with sound if you like.
