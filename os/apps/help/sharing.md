# Screen Sharing
Let someone see and use this computer for a while, or see and use another one.

## Let someone help you
Press Share My Screen. Read them the address, name and password. They connect with any Remote Desktop app: Huginn's, Windows' Remote Desktop Connection, or Microsoft's Windows App on a Mac or phone. The first time, Huginn asks you to allow sharing your screen.

## Stop
Press Stop Sharing, or close the window: sharing never outlasts it. New Password changes the password and disconnects everyone.

## Only share with people you trust
Whoever connects can use your mouse and keyboard.

## Look at another computer
Type its address, the name and the password, then press Connect. For a Windows PC, turn on Remote Desktop in its settings first (Windows Pro and above).

## It won't connect
Both computers need to be on the same network, or reachable over a VPN. If Huginn's firewall is on, allow port 3389.
