# What goes online
Huginn keeps your things on this computer. These are the only times an app talks to the internet, and what it sends.

## Apps that only work with the internet
- **Mail** talks to your mail provider, always encrypted.
- **Messages** talks to your Matrix server. Chats you start are end-to-end encrypted.
- **Weather** sends the coordinates of the places you picked to open-meteo.com. It never works out where you are.
- **Maps** asks OpenStreetMap for map pictures, the places you search for and routes.
- **Podcasts** fetches each show's own feed. Finding a show by name sends the words you typed to Apple's public podcast directory.
- **Feeds** fetches each site's feed directly, with no service in between.
- **App Store** talks to Flathub.
- **Translate** goes online only to fetch a language pack. What you translate never leaves the computer.

## Apps that never go online
Files, Notes, Documents, Writer, Sheets, Slides, Passwords, Authenticator, Contacts, Calendar, Money, Photos, Books, Calculator, Characters, Fonts and Shortcuts keep everything on this computer.
