# Calendar
Your days, weeks and months, kept on this computer.

## Add an event
Pick a day and add an event. Choose how often it repeats: every day, week, month or year, and more patterns from calendar files.

## Week or month
The island at the top right switches between Week and Month; Today jumps back to now.

## Invite people
The envelope button on an event starts a message in Mail that goes out as a real invitation.

## Calendar files
Opening a .ics file from Files, Mail or the browser adds it. Times are changed to this computer's time zone.
