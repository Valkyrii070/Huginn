# Reminders
Things to do, said the way you'd say them.

## Add one
Type it as you'd say it: "Call Sam tomorrow 9am", "rent 1 dec", "in 2 weeks at noon". Huginn works out the time.

## Lists and views
Today, Scheduled, All, Flagged and Done sit beside your own lists.

## Repeats and putting off
A repeating reminder moves to its next time when you tick it. Put one off by an hour, to this evening, tomorrow or next week.

## Notices
A reminder announces itself when it's due, even with the app closed.
