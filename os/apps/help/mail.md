# Mail
All your email accounts in one place, encrypted on the way and with trackers never loaded.

## Add an account
Open Accounts and type your address. Mail knows the usual providers. Google and Microsoft accounts sign in on the provider's own page; iCloud, Yahoo, AOL and Fastmail need an app password, which you make on the provider's website. Sign-ins are kept in your vault, with Passwords.

## Write, reply and forward
Press the write button, or Reply, Reply All or Forward on a message. Drop files on the message to attach them. A message you haven't sent is kept if you close the window.

## Offline
A message written without a connection waits in the Outbox and goes out when the server answers.

## Invitations
An invitation in a message shows Accept, Maybe and Decline. Accept and Maybe put it in Calendar too.

## Keep getting mail with the window closed
Closing the window leaves Mail checking quietly; the dock tile shows how many are unread. Ctrl+Q quits it. "Check when closed" in Accounts turns this off.

## Why some emails look plain
Mail shows emails as text and never fetches anything inside a message, so senders can't tell when you read it.
