# Fonts
Every typeface on this computer, setting your own words.

## Try your words
Type at the top and every font sets them. The slider changes the size.

## Look closer
Click a font to see all its styles, sizes and letters.

## Add a font
Download a font (.ttf, .otf, or a .zip from a font website), then press Add Fonts or drop it on the window. Try it before you install it. Fonts you install are only for you and need no password.

## Remove a font
Only fonts you installed can be removed. Apps that are open may keep showing it until they restart.
