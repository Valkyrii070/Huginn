# Money
See where your money goes, from your bank's own statement files. Nothing is linked to your bank.

## Add your accounts
Press Add an Account for each bank account, card or savings pot.

## Bring in a statement
Download a statement from your bank's website as CSV, OFX or QFX, then press Bring In a Statement. Transactions you already have are skipped.

## Categories
Money guesses a category for each payment. Change one and tick "Always put…" to file that shop the same way from then on.

## Plan
Set monthly budgets per category and make goals: each goal is a jar that fills as you set money aside. Every Month lists payments that come round again, like subscriptions.

## Safety net
The ring on the overview shows how much of three months' spending your savings would cover.

## Privacy
Money keeps everything in one file only you can read. It never signs in to a bank.
