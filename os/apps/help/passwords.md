# Passwords
Your logins in one vault, shared with the Huginn browser, Mail, Messages and Authenticator.

## The vault and the master password
Everything is locked with one master password, which is never stored anywhere. Don't lose it: nobody can open the vault without it.

## Unlock once
Unlocking Passwords, Mail, Messages, Authenticator or the browser unlocks the others until you press Lock in any of them or an hour passes unused. A switch in Passwords turns this off.

## Add and copy
Add a login with its website. Copy a password and the clipboard is wiped after 30 seconds. Make a random password or a passphrase of words.

## Weak and reused passwords
Passwords flags ones that are weak or used more than once.

## Two-factor codes
If Authenticator has a code for a site, it shows on that login here too.

## Move from another browser
Import a .csv exported from another browser or password manager.
