# Welcome
The short tour you saw the first time you signed in.

## What it shows
Two pages to start: hello, then light or dark and the realm colour (the desktop changes as you pick). Then an island walks round the desktop itself, with a line of light leading it from one part to the next: the dock, the date and Today, the app's menu in the top bar (where every app keeps its settings and help), snapping windows, and the Overview. Each step has a "Try it": do it and the tour moves on by itself, or press Next. Skip ends it at any point.

## See it again
Open Welcome from the apps, or Help. It plays the tour again on your desktop.

## After an update
When an update to Huginn OS brings something new, a short "What's new" tour shows it the first time you sign in afterwards, the same way: an island on the thing itself, and a Try it.
