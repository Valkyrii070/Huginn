# Install Huginn OS
Put Huginn OS on a computer, from the installation drive.

## Before you start
Huginn OS needs about 20 GB. To keep Windows beside it, make room first: in Windows, open Disk Management, right-click the C: drive and choose Shrink Volume. Back up anything important either way.

## The steps
**Welcome**: choose your language, then **Try Huginn OS** (nothing on the computer changes; the installer is in the dock when you're ready) or **Install Huginn OS**. **Keyboard**: pick the layout on your keys and try it. **Network**: join Wi‑Fi if you like; installing doesn't need it. **Disk**: choose the disk and how to use it. **You**: your name, user name, password and the computer's name. **Time zone**. **Ready** shows it all once more. Nothing on the disk changes until you press Install. The steps already done in the list at the left can be gone back to.

## Erase, beside, or your own partitions
**Erase the disk** gives Huginn OS all of it, and everything on it is deleted. **Install beside** uses the disk's free space and keeps what's there: each time the computer starts you choose which system to start, and Windows and Huginn OS share the disk's EFI partition. **Choose partitions myself** is for when you've made a partition for Huginn OS already: it's formatted, and the EFI partition is shared.

## Encryption
Turn on **Encrypt the disk** and choose a disk password. It's asked for on the boot screen every time the computer starts, before anything else. Keep it safe: without it nothing on the disk can be read, by anyone, including you.

## While it installs
About ten minutes. The line fills as it goes; **Show Details** shows what it's doing. When it's done, press **Restart Now** and take out the installation drive when the screen goes dark.

## If it stops
It says why. **Save the Log** keeps what happened in your home folder, and **Back to the Summary** lets you change something and try again.
