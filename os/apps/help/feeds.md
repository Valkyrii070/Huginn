# Feeds
News from the sites you choose, as a magazine of story cards, with nothing tracking what you read.

## Follow a site
Press Follow and type the site's address. Feeds finds its feed by itself (RSS, Atom or JSON Feed). Each site gets its own colour, on its cards and its chip.

## Read
Click a card to read the story in one wide, calm column. A− and A+ change the letter size, and Feeds remembers it. All Stories (or Escape) goes back. Open in Huginn takes you to the site in the browser.

## Today, Everything and each site
Today shows what you haven't read yet, newest first, with the newest as the big card. Everything shows all stories; the coloured chips show one site at a time.

## Caught up
Mark All as Read clears today's cards. Star stories you want to keep.

## Move from another reader
In your sources (the list button), Bring In… takes an OPML file from another feed reader, and Save a Copy writes your list to Documents.

## Privacy
Feeds fetches each site's feed directly. Pictures and scripts inside stories are never loaded.
